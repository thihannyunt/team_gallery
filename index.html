    Controls

    W/A/S/D: Move around

    Mouse: Look around

    Space: Toggle pointer lock

    M: Show Menu

    Enter: Start exploration

    Esc: Stop exploration

    G: Play music

    P: Stop music

    V: Switch 1st / 3rd person

    E: Sit down / stand up

    Joystick: Move around

    Drag: Look around

    Tap painting: View photo

    ☰ Menu: Show Menu

    👁: Switch 1st / 3rd person

    🔊: Music on / off
